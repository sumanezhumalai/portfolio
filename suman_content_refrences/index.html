About Me

I didn’t exactly choose computer science; life somehow drifted me toward it, almost like a career destiny I never saw coming. Somewhere along the way, though, curiosity took over. I like exploring things deeply — whether it’s Linux, chess, avionics and military aircraft, history, or the complicated threads of geopolitics.
