# About

I didn’t exactly choose computer science; life somehow drifted me toward it, almost like a career destiny I never saw coming. Somewhere along the way, though, curiosity took over. I like exploring things deeply — whether it’s Linux, chess, avionics and military aircraft, history, or the complicated threads of geopolitics. I have a bit of an obsession with getting things right, and I’ll happily chase a tiny improvement just because I know it can be better. At the same time, I’m stepping into the professional world with plenty of doubt about what lies ahead. But I’m hopeful enough to take the next step anyway. I know I’m nowhere near perfection, and probably never will be; I can only do so much, so while I can, I want to do the utmost with what I have. More than anything, I’m trying to become someone who can stand independently on my own while still being someone the people around me can depend on.
